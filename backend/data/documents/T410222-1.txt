# STUDY_ID: T410222-1
# COMPOUND: BAY-77-C
# SPECIES: RAT
# ROUTE: Dietary admixture
# TITLE: 26-Week Chronic Oral Toxicity Study of BAY-77-C in Wistar Rats

@@PAGE 1
@@SECTION 1 Summary
BAY-77-C was administered continuously via dietary admixture to Wistar Han rats for 26 weeks
at target doses of 0, 5, 15, and 45 mg/kg/day. The NOAEL was 15 mg/kg/day. The principal
target organs were the thyroid and liver.

@@PAGE 52
@@SECTION 3.6 Clinical Pathology
Serum thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) was mildly elevated at 45 mg/kg/day, consistent with
the thyroid histopathology. Total thyroxine (T4) showed a slight decrease at the high dose.
Liver enzymes were minimally elevated at 15 and 45 mg/kg/day.

@@PAGE 78
@@SECTION 3.8 Histopathology
Thyroid follicular cell hypertrophy was observed at 45 mg/kg/day in both sexes, graded minimal
to mild. This finding is consistent with a well-characterized rodent-specific mechanism of
enhanced thyroid hormone clearance secondary to hepatic enzyme induction and is generally
considered of limited relevance to human risk assessment. Centrilobular hepatocellular
hypertrophy was present at 15 and 45 mg/kg/day. No neoplastic changes were observed.

@@PAGE 90
@@SECTION 4 Conclusion
The NOAEL was 15 mg/kg/day. Thyroid follicular cell hypertrophy at 45 mg/kg/day was attributed
to a rodent-specific adaptive mechanism.
