# STUDY_ID: T700555-1
# COMPOUND: BAY-92-D
# SPECIES: RAT
# ROUTE: Inhalation (nose-only)
# TITLE: 4-Week Inhalation Toxicity Study of BAY-92-D in Sprague-Dawley Rats

@@PAGE 1
@@SECTION 1 Summary
BAY-92-D was administered to Sprague-Dawley rats by nose-only inhalation for 4 weeks at
target aerosol concentrations of 0, 50, 150, and 450 mg/m3, 6 hours/day, 5 days/week. The
NOAEC (no-observed-adverse-effect concentration) was 50 mg/m3, based on respiratory tract
findings at higher concentrations.

@@PAGE 34
@@SECTION 3.2 Clinical Signs
Laboured breathing and reduced activity were observed at 450 mg/m3 during and shortly after
exposure. No treatment-related clinical signs were observed at 50 or 150 mg/m3.

@@PAGE 66
@@SECTION 3.8 Histopathology
Squamous metaplasia of the laryngeal epithelium was observed at 150 and 450 mg/m3, graded
minimal to moderate, at the base of the epiglottis. Increased absolute and relative lung
weights were recorded at 450 mg/m3, correlating with alveolar macrophage accumulation. The
larynx is a recognized sensitive site for inhaled particulates in the rat; the finding was
considered a local adaptive/irritant response. No systemic target organ toxicity was observed.

@@PAGE 80
@@SECTION 4 Conclusion
The NOAEC was 50 mg/m3, based on laryngeal squamous metaplasia at 150 mg/m3 and above.
